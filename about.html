Our Origin & Vision

Crafting Resilient Spaces With Zero Compromise

P & S Security Solutions was founded in 2014 by a team of systems architects and telecommunications engineers who recognized a critical gap in the market: security systems were clunky and fragmented, while emerging smart home products were unreliable and vulnerable to cyber exploits.

We set out to unite both worlds into a single, cohesive discipline. Every camera angle we position is ray-traced for optimal field of view. Every network cable is certified Cat6A or optical fiber. Every climate sensor communicates over segregated, cyber-hardened VLANs.
