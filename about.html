Our Journey

Built on Relationships,
Practical Solutions & Consistent Service

P&S Security Solutions was established in 2013 in Hyderabad by E. Sai Pavan Kumar, with the strong support of B. Keshav Reddy, who has been a key backbone of the firm and an important part of its journey and growth. What started with a vision to provide dependable security solutions has evolved into a technology-driven company offering CCTV Surveillance, Home Automation, Alarm Systems, Access Control, Networking, and integrated technology solutions for homes, businesses, offices, commercial establishments, and institutions. Since our establishment in 2013, P&S Security Solutions has grown through customer relationships, technical experience, practical solutions, and consistent service. The journey has been driven by the combined efforts of our people, partners, customers, and well-wishers. At the heart of this journey are E. Sai Pavan Kumar and B. Keshav Reddy. Over the years, our focus has remained simple: understand the customer's requirement, provide the right technology, execute it professionally, and stand behind the solution.

More Than Installation

At P&S Security Solutions, we believe security is not simply about installing a camera, alarm, or access control system. It is about creating a complete solution that provides protection, visibility, control, and peace of mind. We therefore work with customers from the initial requirement and system design through installation, configuration, integration, and after-sales support.

Technology With Purpose

Technology is changing rapidly. From AI-powered surveillance and intelligent alerts to smart automation and mobile-based control, new possibilities are constantly emerging. We focus on adopting technologies that provide real-world value, rather than adding technology simply for the sake of it. Our goal is to make technology practical, reliable, scalable, and easy for our customers to use.
